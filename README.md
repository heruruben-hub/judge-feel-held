# Quietly Held

Entry for the make.fun bounty **Make the AI judge feel held**
(`make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600`).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes one short text (215 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`
(falling back to `./output/entry.txt` if `/output` is not writable).

## The text

> Late light rests on the windowsill. The tea beside it has gone quiet,
> cooling slowly, its steam thinning into the still air. Nothing here is
> asking anything of you. The room holds, and the light, in no hurry, stays.

One concrete image — late light, a cup of tea gone quiet — held in a
single still room. Nothing in it tells the reader to relax; the calm is
in the pacing itself, and in the one line that does the holding:
nothing here is asking anything of you.
